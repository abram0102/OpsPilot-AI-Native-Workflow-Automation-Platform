# Acme Demo — Shipping and Delivery Policy (fictional)

Acme Demo is a fictional company invented for this project's demo. Nothing in this document
describes a real business, a real customer or a real shipment.

## Delivery windows

Standard delivery is 3–5 business days within the contiguous United States. Express delivery is
1–2 business days. Orders placed after 14:00 local time ship the following business day.

## When tracking has not updated

Tracking information can stop updating for up to 72 hours while a parcel moves between carrier
hubs. This is normal and does not mean the parcel is lost.

A parcel is treated as delayed once tracking has not updated for **six business days**. At that
point support should:

1. Open a carrier trace request on the customer's behalf.
2. Tell the customer a trace has been opened and that carrier traces take up to 5 business days.
3. Offer either a replacement shipment or a full refund if the trace does not resolve.

## Refunds for late delivery

Express orders that arrive more than two business days late are refunded the shipping charge
automatically on request. Standard orders are not, because the 3–5 day window is an estimate.
