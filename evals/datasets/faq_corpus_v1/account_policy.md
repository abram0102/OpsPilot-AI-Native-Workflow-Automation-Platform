# Northwind Supply Co. — Account FAQ

All content here is fictional and exists only to exercise the knowledge indexer.

## I cannot sign in to my account.

The most common cause is an email address that differs from the one the account was opened with.
A password reset link is sent to the registered address and stays valid for one hour. Requesting a
second link immediately invalidates the first.

## My password reset email never arrived.

Reset emails are sent within a minute. If one has not arrived, it is usually because the address
has no account attached to it, since we deliberately do not reveal whether an address is
registered. Checking the spam folder is worthwhile before trying another address.

## How do I enable two-factor authentication?

Two-factor authentication is enabled from the security section of the account page and uses an
authenticator app. Recovery codes are shown once at setup and cannot be retrieved later, so they
should be stored somewhere safe before leaving that page.

## I am locked out after too many attempts.

An account locks for fifteen minutes after ten consecutive failed sign-in attempts. The lock clears
on its own; support cannot shorten it, and a successful password reset does not bypass it.

## How do I close my account?

An account can be closed from the account page. Closing removes access immediately, while order
records are retained for the period required by law before deletion.
