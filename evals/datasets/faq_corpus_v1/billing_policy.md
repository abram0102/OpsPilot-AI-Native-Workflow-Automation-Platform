# Northwind Supply Co. — Billing FAQ

All content here is fictional and exists only to exercise the knowledge indexer.

## When am I charged for an order?

A card is authorised when the order is placed and captured when the parcel is dispatched. An
authorisation that is never captured falls away on its own, typically within seven days, though the
exact timing is set by the card issuer rather than by us.

## I was charged twice for the same order.

A duplicate charge is almost always a pending authorisation sitting alongside the real capture.
If both entries are still present after seven working days, the duplicate is genuine and is
refunded in full on request. Refunds are returned to the original payment method.

## How long does a refund take?

Once a refund is issued it reaches the card within five to ten working days. The delay is entirely
on the issuing bank's side; nothing further is required from the customer once the refund shows as
issued on the order page.

## Can I get an invoice for my order?

An itemised invoice is available on the order page as soon as the order is dispatched. Invoices for
business accounts include the registered company details captured at checkout.

## How do I cancel a subscription?

A subscription can be cancelled at any time from the account page. Cancelling stops the next
renewal; the current period remains active until its end date and is not refunded pro rata.
