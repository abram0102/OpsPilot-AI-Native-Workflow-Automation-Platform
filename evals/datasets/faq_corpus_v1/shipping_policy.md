# Northwind Supply Co. — Shipping FAQ

All content here is fictional and exists only to exercise the knowledge indexer.

## How long does delivery take?

Standard delivery within the mainland takes three to five working days from the moment the parcel
leaves our warehouse. Express delivery arrives the next working day when the order is placed before
14:00. Orders placed after that cut-off are picked the following morning.

## Where is my tracking number?

A tracking number is emailed as soon as the courier scans the parcel at our warehouse, which is
usually a few hours after the order is confirmed. If twenty-four hours have passed with no tracking
email, the parcel has most likely not been collected yet, and contacting support is worthwhile.

## What happens if a parcel is lost in transit?

A parcel is treated as lost once the courier has not scanned it for ten consecutive working days.
At that point a replacement is dispatched at no cost, or a full refund is issued, whichever the
customer prefers. We do not require the customer to contact the courier themselves.

## Can I change the delivery address after ordering?

The delivery address can be changed while the order still shows as "preparing". Once the parcel has
been collected by the courier the address is fixed, and the parcel must be redirected through the
courier's own service.

## Do you deliver outside the mainland?

Island and offshore deliveries take an additional two working days and are not eligible for the
express option. Remote postcodes are listed in the checkout page's delivery notice.
